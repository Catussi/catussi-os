## Thesis · Academic Pathways

**Machine learning applied to academic trajectories**

The thesis analyzed longitudinal student records with supervised models. I prepared the data, compared algorithms, and used SHAP to study how each variable contributed to predictions.

The focus wasn't only on getting a strong metric. I also reviewed interpretability and possible bias, because in education a prediction can influence decisions that affect a person. The defense received a grade of **6.6 out of 7.0**.

The data and repository are not public because this is academic research.
