## About me

I'm a Computer Science Engineer graduated from Universidad Andrés Bello. My most recent experience was at ITISB, where I spent six months on ELVIR — a job-interview prep platform built for young people from Teletón.

I joined when the idea still needed shaping: I made mockups, defined flows, proposed the data model, and helped decide how to split frontend, API, and simulation sessions. ## How I work

I usually research thoroughly before implementing. I like to understand the problem, review the constraints, and leave a record of decisions so someone else can continue the work. On ELVIR that meant keeping architecture docs, API contracts, an entity-relationship model, deployment notes, and user manuals — in addition to the code.

I use AI tools to speed up concrete tasks, but I verify what they produce and keep judgment over the solution. When I don't know a technology, I'd rather say so, study it, and build a small proof of concept before committing it to the project.

I'm also working intentionally on communicating more clearly and performing better under pressure. I'm consistent, I learn quickly when I have a clear goal, and I'm comfortable collaborating with both technical and non-technical people.

## Experience that shaped me most

### ELVIR · ITISB / Teletón

ELVIR is a virtual job coach with an avatar and artificial intelligence. The platform lets someone prepare for an interview, run a simulation, and review the process from different roles: young person, tutor, and administrator.

My work covered frontend, backend, database, authentication, permissions, LiveAvatar/LiveKit integration, Spanish voice with ElevenLabs, testing, documentation, and deployment. I also helped resolve issues that show up in a real pilot: audio permissions, mobile compatibility, waiting states, and support during the trial.

> The result was an install-free demo used in a pilot with Teletón. Moving from intern to junior developer on the same project is one of the milestones I value most.
